# Water

Reminds you to drink water. Every 15 minutes the notch opens with **Snooze** and **Drank** buttons.

## How it works

- **Drank** logs one glass (250 ml) and starts the next reminder.
- **Snooze** pushes the reminder back (10 minutes by default).
- A reminder pops up once and waits for your answer.
- The page shows today's total against your goal, with a ring and an hourly chart.

## Settings (on the page, press Enter to save)

- **Every**: minutes between reminders (1 to 240)
- **Snooze**: snooze length in minutes (1 to 120)
- **Goal**: daily goal in ml (100 to 10000)
- **From / To**: active hours, as HH:MM

## Data

Saved in `%APPDATA%\Fenn\plugin-data\water\water.json`.
