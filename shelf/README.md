# Shelf

A shelf for the texts you copy again and again: prompts, replies, links, snippets. Save each one once, then copy it from the notch in one click.

## How to use

1. Click the Shelf tile to open it.
2. Click **New**, type a **Title** and the **Text to copy**, then click **Save**.
3. Every saved item shows its title and a short preview, with three buttons:
   - **Copy** puts the full text on your clipboard.
   - **Edit** lets you change the title or the text.
   - **Delete** removes the item straight away.

The tile shows how many items you have saved.

## Note

Fenn's text fields are single-line, so line breaks in pasted text may not be kept. Long texts are fine.

## Data

Items are saved in `%APPDATA%\Fenn\plugin-data\shelf\shelf.json`.
